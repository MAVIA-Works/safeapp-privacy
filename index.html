SafetyFirst Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026. Developer: emberrose. Contact: Mavia0826@gmail.com. Applies to the SafetyFirst Android app.

What stays on your device

SafetyFirst locks apps you choose with your fingerprint or app PIN. The list of locked apps is stored encrypted on your device only. Your fingerprint is handled by Android; the app only receives a yes/no result. If you set an optional app PIN, it is stored only on your device as a protected hash, never as the PIN itself. The app uses "Usage access" to detect, in real time on your device, when a locked app opens. That information is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Permissions

Usage access and Display over other apps (to detect and cover locked apps), Biometric (to verify you), Foreground service and Notifications (to keep protection running), Run at startup (to restart after reboot), and Internet (for ads and your subscription).

Ads and subscription

The free version shows Google AdMob ads. AdMob may use device identifiers (such as advertising ID), IP address and ad interaction data. SafetyFirst itself does not use your location for any feature, but AdMob may collect approximate location (for example, derived from your IP address) to show and measure ads. AdMob and Google may also collect crash logs and diagnostic information, such as device model, Android version and error details, to keep their services stable and secure. Where required (e.g. EEA, UK) we ask for your consent first, and you can change it in the app under "Privacy Policy" > "Ad privacy choices". See Google's policy. Premium (PHP 399 per month, auto-renewing) removes ads and is handled by Google Play; we never receive your card details. Cancel any time in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions. You can also leave an optional tip, paid through Google Play; it unlocks nothing and we never receive your payment details.

Sharing, children and your rights

We do not sell your data and share it only with Google (AdMob and Google Play) as described above. We run no servers holding your personal data and there is no account. The app is not directed to children under 13. Uninstalling deletes all data the app stored on your device.

We will update this page and its date if the policy changes.
